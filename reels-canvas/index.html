Reels Canvas

Friends send reels in batches. Today, watching them means leaving the chat, coming back, and scrolling up again for every single one.

Today, for every reel

  1. Open the chat
  2. Find the reel
  3. Watch it
  4. Back out
  5. Reply
  6. Scroll up again

Swiping up from a friend's reel takes you to a suggested reel, not their next one.

Here, you open the chat once. Sam's reels play back to back with Sam's message attached, and you reply without leaving.

↑ ↓Next reel from Sam. After Sam, the rest of your feed, one topic per row.
← →Go deeper into whatever is on screen: same topic, same event. Swipe back to return to Sam.
  • Every swipe runs on a spring and follows your finger, so you can catch a reel mid-flight and throw it back.
  • Sideways is a push, not a scroll. The new reel slides over the old one, so going deeper feels different from moving on.
  • Sam's message pops in like a story caption, so the reason they sent it stays with the reel.
  • A mini-map keeps you oriented once the feed moves in two directions.
